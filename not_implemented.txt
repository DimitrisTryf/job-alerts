Implementation requirement
--------------------------

A source can move out of this file only after a live employer-controlled job or
ATS response is verified to contain a true publication field such as
datePosted, postedDate, first_published, publishedAt, or releasedDate.

Search-engine timestamps, modification dates, application deadlines, and the
time Job Watch first detects a listing are not acceptable substitutes.
